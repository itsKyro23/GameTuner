Privacy Policy — Game Tuner

This Privacy Policy explains how Game Tuner handles information when you use the application. We are committed to maintaining transparency and protecting user privacy.

1. Information Collection

Game Tuner does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to its own servers. The app does not require user registration, and all core functionality operates locally on the device.

The application integrates third-party advertising services, which may collect certain non-personal information as described below.

2. Permissions Used

The app may use the following Android permissions:

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES – Used to display installed applications.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – Used to detect network availability.
  • INTERNET – Required to load advertising content.
  • AD_ID – Used for advertising identification purposes.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

Game Tuner uses Google AdMob as its primary advertising platform. Advertising may be delivered through AdMob mediation partners, including Unity Ads.

These services may process non-personal information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device model and operating system version
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data

Data handling is subject to the respective privacy policies of these third-party providers.
